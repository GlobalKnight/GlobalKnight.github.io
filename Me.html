About Me

I recently graduated from DigiPen Institute of Technology with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Game Design. I am constantly motivated by what I could do better, searching relentlessly for new ways to create games that somebody could love playing. I worked on multiple teams and grew through teamwork and struggles that we could solve together. Whether these struggles came through technical issues in the engines we used, or even bigger issues with game direction. For every project, I worked hard to ensure that our team would always make it to the end. These teams ranged from groups as small as three members upwards to twenty-two, filled with multiple disciplines striving for the same goal. All the games I helped to create and the projects I worked on independently were vastly different from each other. All were built upon with different focuses on mechanics that were iterated through and built personally by people who truly cared about their craft.

Using Unreal, I helped create systems and gameplay for a world filled with massive and unique Moths. Projects I built in Unity helped me learn Technical Design skills that increased my ability to solve problems and the roles I can work within and understand. This iteration and uniqueness were the parts I loved the most, and creating something that could be deeply enjoyed and honestly slightly addictive to experience became the most important thing to me.
